# Council growth report

Thimblehaven council now explains the current economy, housing capacity and
practical growth blockers. Expand Growth forecast & what to do next to see the
next collection's projected household savings, levy revenue, arrivals/departures
and construction spending. Forecasts run the same town simulation as actual
play against an immutable copy, assuming unchanged market and safety conditions.
They never award coins, create residents or advance the live town.

Advice identifies full housing, weak employment, enemy pressure, damaged
buildings and levy relief. It explains that reducing levies helps savings but
does not control the market cycle. Existing prices and save data are unchanged.
Six new checks compare forecasts to real simulation and cover purity, projects,
housing, household budgets, downturns and countdowns. Android version 12,
0.1.11-town-outlook.
