# People make a town

Thimblehaven now has eight persistent neighbour personalities, favourite common
gifts and regional home styles. The council's Neighbours tab shows friendship,
local visits, inventory gifts and letters. Clicking a resident or home opens that
resident's profile. Walking buttons use the existing land route planner.

Say hello locally to introduce yourself. First introductions earn 4 friendship;
later conversations earn 2 after 4 active town ticks. Favourite gifts earn 6,
other gifts earn 2, consuming exactly one carrot, sprat or loaf. Gifts require
an introduction and 10 active town ticks between gifts. Aquarium fish and rare
species are never consumed. Friendship does not decay offline. Dialogue refers
to gifts, community work, local construction, personal quirks and friendly rivals.

After an introduction, a finished home can be visited locally. The room uses the
existing walkable cottage furniture/navigation with a host, personal rug and wall
art. Its door returns outside without changing the player's coordinates. A first
housewarming earns 6 friendship once. Non-friends cannot visit at night; close
neighbours can. Interactions cannot bypass combat, sailing, rescues or transport.
The player's cottage tea, upgrades and inventory services are not offered here.

Friendship thresholds send one letter each. Move-ins, finished home work, public
openings and departures also send letters. The mailbox retains up to 32 messages;
reading them never creates currency. Memories remain if a resident leaves and
returns. Home plots are keyed by resident identity, so departures do not shift
other neighbours' houses around the map. Vacated homes remain standing and can
be reused when a family returns; they do not pay for the same first home twice.

Town projects now take 6–16 existing world ticks after one upfront coin payment.
The service and its milestone prerequisite activate on completion, rather than
on funding. The council shows progress and the world shows builders and scaffold
stages. Existing completed projects stay completed when older saves load.

New families pay 40 coins of their 80-coin starting purse for a first home, which
builds over 4 ticks while they wait with luggage. Existing households save towards
extensions: level 2 costs 120, level 3 costs 320, with at least 40 coins left over.
These require the cottages/hall and a healthy economy; no player coins are used.
Extensions take 6/10 ticks. Paid work continues to completion even if the market
later weakens. Active game simulation advances these stages; title screens,
interiors, fights, overseas visits and hidden tabs pause them. No offline bursts.

Save version 16 preserves work progress, home levels, friendships and letters.
Legacy households retain finished homes, including the earlier town-hall upgrade. Construction and mail are
validated on load. Automated checks cover one-time funding, pause rules, gifts,
local interaction restrictions, repeat visits, letters, NPC budgets, new families,
stable addresses, migration and walkable room furniture.
